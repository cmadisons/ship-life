# Ship Life — the list

Kept, not thrown away. Four lists of twenty-five: things to add, things to
take away, things to change, and things to make better. Cross one off when it
is built rather than deleting it, and add to it whenever something comes up.

What is **not** on here, because it is already decided and built: what a friend
does (they give you gear — Ben the armour and the bombs, Izzy the boots and the
rest of the set), and what floor 16 is (the friends' room, Izzy's).
