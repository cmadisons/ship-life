# Ship Life -- Quest Lists

Every quest in here is one the mod can watch you finish. That is the whole
rule for this file: a quest the game cannot see you do is a checkbox you
tick yourself, so the six hundred and fifty written from the design -- turn
on your TV, look in the mirror, sit on your bed -- are gone, and what is
left is what `QuestPool.java` and `QuestDay.java` actually hand out.

The eleven things the game counts:

| Counter | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| `SNAKE_FOOD` | food eaten in Snake |
| `GALAGA_ROUNDS` | rounds passed in Galaga |
| `ARCADE` | arcade tickets earned |
| `LAPS` | laps swum |
| `PETS` | pets owned |
| `WAVES` | waves cleared on floor 9 |
| `RACES` | races finished |
| `BOSSES` | bosses beaten |
| `EVENT` | event tickets earned |
| `MONEY` | dollars |
| `PACMAN_BEST` | your Pac-Man record |

---

## Quest Day

On every day of a week that has no other event in it. Four quests -- a super
easy, an easy, a medium and a hard -- and **500 event tickets** for finishing
all four, then a fresh set of four worth another 500, for as long as the day
lasts. One finished quest is also what opens floor 10.

| Tier | Drawn from |
| --- | --- |
| Super easy | 3 food in Snake · 1 Galaga round · 1 lap · 3 arcade tickets |
| Easy | 10 food · 3 rounds · 3 laps · 10 tickets · 1 pet |
| Medium | 25 food · 6 rounds · 8 laps · 40 tickets · Pac-Man 15 |
| Hard | 60 food · 12 rounds · 20 laps · 120 tickets · Pac-Man 30 |

---
